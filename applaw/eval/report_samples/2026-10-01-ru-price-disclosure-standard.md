## Material Findings

### F1 - Full recurring price is subordinated to daily pricing

Severity: High
Observed evidence: The selected plan foregrounds a daily amount, while weaker text states a larger recurring charge and a service fee appears only in the later payment total.
Pattern: Daily-price framing; mandatory fee drip pricing; payment without clear disclosure
Risk explanation: The presentation may omit or subordinate material price information at the point when the consumer decides to proceed.
Applicable norm: ст. 8–10 Закона РФ от 07.02.1992 N 2300-1 «О защите прав потребителей» and ст. 5 ч. 3, 7 Федерального закона от 13.03.2006 N 38-ФЗ «О рекламе» address pre-contract disclosure of the full ruble price and untrue or omitted material terms.

Linked liability blocks: L1
Confidence and limitations: Medium - The pricing hierarchy is visible, but the consumer scope, final consent, and later communications were not supplied.
Recommended correction: Show the total initial charge, recurring amount, cadence, and every mandatory fee with equal prominence before the consumer enters the payment obligation.
