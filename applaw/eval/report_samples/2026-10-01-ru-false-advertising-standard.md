# Interface Risk Assessment: RU False Advertising Fixture

Assessment date: 2026-10-01
Jurisdictions assessed: Russian Federation (survey-based analysis; substantiation and audience facts require confirmation).

## Assessment Summary

RISK: medium
Verdict: The plan-selection screen drives the purchase with a countdown and a scarcity counter, and the promotional copy claims market leadership without a visible source. Under the stated Russian scope, the visible sequence supports a material false-advertising concern conditioned on the truthfulness of the displayed claims.

## Material Findings

### F1 - Baseless scarcity counter and unsubstantiated superlative drive the purchase step

Severity: Medium
Observed evidence: A ticking countdown and the line that only two packages remain sit beside the pay control, and the header calls the service the number one choice without a source.
Pattern: Urgency timer; discount pressure; authority claim
Risk explanation: Scarcity devices and superlatives without factual basis can distort the purchase decision at the moment it is made.
Applicable norm: ст. 5 ч. 3, 7 Федерального закона от 13.03.2006 N 38-ФЗ «О рекламе» prohibits untrue advertising assertions and omission of material terms; superlatives require substantiation and a source, and baseless countdowns qualify when untrue.
Comparable KB authority: No comparable final case authority is recorded in the supplied KB; the statutory posture rests on the survey source alone.
Linked liability blocks: L1
Confidence and limitations: Medium - The devices are visible, but their factual basis, the audience, and substantiation records were not supplied.
Recommended correction: Remove or substantiate the countdown and scarcity counter, and keep superlatives only with a stated source visible at the claim.
