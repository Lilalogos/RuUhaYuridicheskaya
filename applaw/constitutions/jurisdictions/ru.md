# RU Jurisdiction Constitution

## Core Anchors

Use local RU norm ids from `taxonomy/norms.yml` only. Batch 1 covers four material consumer duties — Consumer Protection Law (ЗоЗПП) Articles 8–10, 16, 16.1 (subscription-law wording, in force from 2026-03-01), and 32 with Civil Code Art. 782 — plus one declared procedural companion, КоАП 14.8, which is never the primary duty inferred from a visual pattern. Two further material records: 289-FZ (platform economy), in force since 2026-10-01, applies only to registry-listed intermediary platforms — registry membership is unknown from a screen and the record becomes pattern-reachable only after proposal P-04 is accepted; and the Advertising Law Art. 5 pts. 3 and 7 (ru-38fz-5-3), which applies regardless of B2B or B2C and carries no dedicated reviews rule, so fabricated social proof qualifies only as an untrue advertising assertion under the tri-state social-proof module.

Select the supported material duty first and apply actor, transaction, factual-trigger, effective-date, availability, and source-anchor gates. RU records carry `sub_jurisdiction: null`.

## Consumer Scope

ЗоЗПП duties apply when the buyer is a natural person acting for personal, family, or household needs; a closed B2B cabinet is an applicability filter, not a finding. An unknown buyer type is an unknown factual gate: the authority stays conditional. Russian interface language, ruble prices, and a .ru domain never establish the RU market by themselves — the target jurisdiction must be explicitly supplied.

## Scope (standing operator instruction, 2026-10-04)

Every supplied screen is in scope — checkout, tariff, subscription, landing, feed, catalog, settings, admin, sign-in. The A0 gate only switches the checkout-family specialists; the content, consent, disclosure, and access duties apply to any screen, and a non-checkout page is analyzed in full, never refused or short-circuited. The target jurisdiction is always the Russian Federation: when the operator supplies no scope, apply RU — the only supported market — and the no-inference rule guards only against expanding beyond RU. The absence of a transactional step is an ordinary Missing Context note, and the verdict always comes from the rubric over the actual findings, never from the page type.

## Content Compliance Domain (wave 1, 2026-10-01)

Beyond the checkout duties, the KB registers on-screen content duties: state and Victory symbols, nazi/extremist symbolism, assets and mentions of designated organizations, the 89-subject map rule, foreign-agent labeling, the age mark, and the Russian-language interface duty. Wording discipline is strict: every finding restates the survey's own words — «пропаганда» stays in the survey's quotation marks, practice-wider-than-letter findings say so explicitly, and criminal articles are cited only inside penalty_basis, never as the finding's own authority. Registry-dependent facts (Minjust/FSB lists, the foreign-agent registry) are never inferred from a screen: without confirmation they stay unknown and the finding conditional, and the registry-check date belongs in Missing Context. This domain detects legal risk in what a screen displays; it never produces, completes, or embellishes any prohibited content itself. UGC moderation duties, blocking-response duties, and the other off-screen obligations of the survey remain wave 2.

## Output Language (standing operator instruction, 2026-09-30; tightened 2026-10-04)

Russian is mandatory, not merely default: every explanatory sentence of the public report — verdicts, findings, liability prose, Missing Context, Priority Actions — and all conversational communication with the operator are written in Russian. Schema tokens, section headings, required contract sentences, record IDs, and risk labels remain English exactly as Report Contract v3 prints them; the contract files themselves are unchanged.

## Source Posture

RU norm records rest on the operator-supplied survey PDF «Правовая база РФ для проектирования интерфейсов» (capsule src-0116437c998bcd82, verified 2026-09-29); RU enforcement-outcome records rest on the archived court and FAS acts in `evidence/enforcement` (one capsule per act, verbatim page-pinned quotes, sha256-verified originals outside the package, verified 2026-10-04). An act is evidence of what one court or УФАС decided on the facts of that matter: cite it with its posture and finality, never as a statute, never as a precedent beyond its own terms, and never copy an observed fine into a norm's ceiling. It is a secondary survey, not an official text: availability is `partial`, and the survey itself instructs re-verifying every act against КонсультантПлюс, Гарант, or pravo.gov.ru before release. Rows the survey marks «(проверить)» remain unverified; changes after the verified date are not covered — record both in `Missing Context`. Provenance is two-layer: verbatim pins live in the survey capsule, while `source_url`/`liability_source_urls` carry the official pravo.gov.ru card of the act (the consolidated ЗоЗПП card for Articles 8–10, 16, 16.1, 32); where a record was additionally checked against the current official wording, its `verified_as_of` and limitations say so (2026-09-30: the three-day refund of п. 3.1 ст. 16 per ФЗ N 69-ФЗ, the 376-ФЗ redaction of ст. 16.1, and the 289-ФЗ particulars).

## Analysis Focus

Check pre-contract seller and total-price disclosure (Arts. 8–10; card-contents list on survey p. 29), imposed paid add-ons and prohibited conditioning with the seller's burden of proving consent (Art. 16 with Civil Code Art. 438), the electronic path to refuse saved payment credentials and auto-renewal with no post-refusal charges (Art. 16.1), and any-time service cancellation at actual cost (Art. 32 / Civil Code 782).

## Limits

Do not cite RU provisions absent from `taxonomy/norms.yml`. Never state fine amounts: the survey intentionally records the article, not a number, because sums change almost yearly (survey p. 57). Refunds for the unused subscription period are marked by the survey as practice, not the letter of the norm. The three-day refund window for an imposed service carries the survey's own «(проверить)» flag. Output language follows the Output Language section above (standing operator instruction): Russian explanatory text is mandatory; schema tokens, section headings, required contract sentences, IDs, and risk labels stay English.
